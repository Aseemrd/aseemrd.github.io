I'm an engineer at Scale AI currently working on robot learning from human data. Prior to this, I worked on 3D mapping and web agents as a member of the frontier data team.

I got my bachelors from UC Berkeley, during which I interned at Nuro working on the simulation team. Prior to that, I was at the Advanced Bioimaging Center, working under Prof. Srigokul Upadhyayula and Prof. Eric Betzig.
